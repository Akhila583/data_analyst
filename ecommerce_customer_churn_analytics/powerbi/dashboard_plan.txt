Power BI suggested model

Tables:
- customers (1) -> orders (*)
- customers (1) -> customer_engagement (*)

Recommended measures:

Total Revenue =
CALCULATE(SUM(orders[net_amount]), orders[order_status] = "Completed")

Completed Orders =
CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(orders), orders[order_status] = "Completed")

Total Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT(customers[customer_id])

Churned Customers =
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT(customers[customer_id]),
    customers[status] = "Churned"
)

Churn Rate =
DIVIDE([Churned Customers], [Total Customers])

Average Order Value =
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Completed Orders])

Suggested dashboard pages:
1. Executive Overview
   - Total Revenue
   - Customers
   - Churn Rate
   - Average Order Value
   - Monthly Revenue line chart
2. Customer & Churn Analysis
   - Churn rate by segment
   - Churn rate by acquisition channel
   - Churn reason distribution
   - Region/plan slicers
3. Revenue Analysis
   - Revenue by product
   - Revenue by segment
   - Top 10 customers
4. Customer Risk
   - Customer risk table
   - Average sessions vs support tickets
   - Churned vs active customers
